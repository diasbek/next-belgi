## 1. Purpose

Belgi.ai automatically compares the entered sign with available registry records and other permitted sources, analyzing verbal, visual and/or semantic features. The result helps conduct a preliminary search and identify objects for further review.

## 2. Limitations

The result is a probabilistic informational assessment. It is not an examination by a state authority, a legal opinion, confirmation of the absence of third-party rights or a guarantee of registration. A registry may be incomplete, update with delay or contain errors; the algorithm may miss a similar sign or show an irrelevant match. The assessment depends on spelling, image, classes, goods list and quality of source data.

## 3. User responsibility

Before filing an application and commercial use, it is recommended to check right holders, applications, trade names, domains, copyright and other rights and, where risk is material, to consult a patent attorney or lawyer. The User must not decide solely on the basis of a score or risk color.
